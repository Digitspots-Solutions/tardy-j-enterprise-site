About Us

Thank You Mart AgriTech Limited (also known as Thank You Mart FarmTech Nigeria) is revolutionizing the Nigerian food supply chain through innovative technology solutions. Based in Karu, Abuja, we are dedicated to eliminating inefficiencies in the farm-to-table journey. Our platform connects smallholder farmers directly with consumers, retailers, and food businesses, reducing waste, ensuring freshness, and creating fair value for all stakeholders.

With deep expertise in food supply chain management, we understand the unique challenges of the Nigerian agricultural sector and have built solutions that work for our local context. We believe that technology can transform how Nigeria feeds itself—making nutritious food accessible and affordable while empowering our farming communities.

Our Services

Farm-to-Consumer Direct Supply

We connect you directly with verified farmers across Nigeria, eliminating middlemen and ensuring you get the freshest produce at fair prices. Track your food from farm to your doorstep.

Agricultural Supply Chain Management

Comprehensive supply chain solutions for businesses, restaurants, and retailers. We handle sourcing, quality control, logistics, and timely delivery of farm produce across Nigeria.

Farmer Empowerment Program

We provide smallholder farmers with access to markets, fair pricing, training on best agricultural practices, and technology tools to improve their yields and incomes.

Fresh Produce Marketplace

Our digital platform offers a wide variety of fresh fruits, vegetables, grains, and other farm products delivered directly to homes and businesses in Abuja and surrounding areas.

Quality Assurance & Food Safety

Every product in our supply chain undergoes rigorous quality checks. We maintain cold chain logistics and ensure food safety standards are met from harvest to delivery.

B2B Agricultural Solutions

Tailored supply solutions for hotels, restaurants, supermarkets, and food processing companies requiring consistent quality and reliable delivery schedules.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order fresh produce from Thank You Mart?
You can order through our website at thankyoumart.org, call us at 07068179182, or message us on WhatsApp. We'll confirm your order and schedule delivery based on your location in Abuja and surrounding areas.
What areas do you deliver to?
We currently deliver across Abuja, Karu, and neighboring communities. For businesses requiring regular bulk supply, we can arrange deliveries to other locations in North Central Nigeria.
How do you ensure the quality of produce?
All our partner farmers are verified and trained in quality standards. We conduct inspections at the farm level, maintain proper storage and cold chain logistics, and perform final quality checks before delivery.
Can I visit your farms?
Yes! We organize farm tours for interested consumers and businesses. Contact us to schedule a visit and see firsthand how your food is grown and our quality processes in action.
Do you work with smallholder farmers?
Absolutely. Supporting smallholder farmers is central to our mission. We provide them with market access, fair pricing, training, and technology tools to succeed.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers, card payments, and mobile money. For businesses, we offer flexible payment terms based on order volume and frequency.
How quickly can I get my order delivered?
For consumers, we typically deliver within 24-48 hours. Businesses on our regular supply program receive deliveries according to their scheduled arrangements.
Is there a minimum order quantity?
For individual consumers, we have flexible minimum orders. Businesses and bulk buyers can discuss custom arrangements based on their needs.
